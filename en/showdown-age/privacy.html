Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are

This policy explains what data the game Showdown Age uses and why. The game is made by DC General Games Studio, a studio of DC General Electric LLC (United States). If you have questions, write to the email address at the end.

2. What data we use

  • Your account. When you first open the game we create a guest account with a number that identifies your phone inside the game. If you choose to save your progress, we also store your email, your username and your password (the password is stored encrypted).
  • Your game. Your town, your heroes, your resources, your battles and your in-game purchases.
  • What you write. Chat and clan messages. Other players in that chat can see them.
  • Notifications. If you allow them, a Firebase number to send notifications to your phone, your language and your time zone. The time zone is only the hour difference from world time: we do not use your location.
  • Technical data. IP address and connection logs on our servers, to keep the game running and to detect cheating and abuse.

3. Why we use it

  • So you can play online and keep your progress.
  • To send you notifications (attacks, finished upgrades). You can turn them off in the game Settings or in Android.
  • To keep the game fair: to detect cheating, fake accounts and purchase fraud.
  • To answer you if you write to us.

4. Purchases

Real-money purchases are charged by Google Play. We never see your card details: Google only sends us the purchase confirmation, so we can deliver what you bought.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Only these services use it, to make the game work:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, to deliver notifications to your phone.
  • Google Play, to charge and confirm purchases.
  • The provider that runs our servers, which stores accounts and games.

If the law requires it, we may have to give data to an authority.

6. How long we keep it

As long as you have your account. If you log out, we delete that phone's notification number. If you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep (for example, purchase records).

7. Delete your account

In the game: Settings → Account → «Delete account». We ask you to confirm twice. Your account and data are deleted 30 days later; if you sign in again before then, the deletion is canceled. You can also ask by email:

To delete your account and your data, write to us from your account email (or tell us your username) with the subject «Delete my account». We will confirm when it is done. Deleting your account cannot be undone and does not refund purchases.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 gave us data, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it. Depending on where you live, the law may give you other rights; write to us and we will help.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above. If the change is important, we also tell you inside the game.

11. Contact

Email: dcgeneralgamesstudio@gmail.com
